ABOUT RAILKIT

Modern tooling for a very old network.

Indian Railways moves more people every day than most countries have citizens, but the data around it — PNR status, live running, fares, station charts — usually comes back as dense, unreadable JSON. Railkit is a lightweight utility layer that fetches that data and renders it the way a human actually wants to read it: clear labels, color-coded status, and zero guesswork.
